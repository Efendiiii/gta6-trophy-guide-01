    Roadmap to Platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions. One playthrough is enough, there is no mission replay and no New Game+, and free roam continues after the story. Launch-era estimates put the platinum at roughly 200 hours, difficulty about 4/10 (PowerPyx). Never use cheats — they disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx, GameSpot). There are no online trophies.

      Order at a glance

        Missable trophies

        Only one trophy is flagged as missable by the launch-era guides (PowerPyx, GTABase). Everything else the guides describe can still be earned in free roam after the story. The warning below is the one that matters — do it early.

        Not missable — but do not leave them all to the end

        GTABase warns that some stranger missions are only offered for a while. The side-character final missions (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) and the 8 stranger strands are listed here so you can keep an eye on them while you play.
